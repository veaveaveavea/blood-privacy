# Blood Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 7, 2026**

Blood is a Root community bot providing moderation, economy, gambling, VoiceMaster, and utility features.

## Information Blood May Store

Blood may store limited information necessary to provide its features, including:

- Root user and community identifiers
- Economy balances and inventory data
- Gambling statistics and game-related data
- Moderation records, warnings, cases, and reasons
- Command aliases and community configuration
- Name history observed by Blood
- VoiceMaster configuration and temporary channel information
- Welcome, goodbye, logging, and other bot settings

## How Information Is Used

Information is used only to operate Blood's features, maintain community settings, provide moderation functionality, and operate economy and game systems.

Blood does not sell user information.

## Data Sharing

Blood does not sell or intentionally provide stored user information to advertisers or data brokers.

Information may be processed through Root and infrastructure necessary to operate the bot.

## Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide Blood's features. Temporary information may be cleared when the bot restarts or when it is no longer needed.

## Security

Reasonable efforts are made to protect information handled by Blood. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Blood's features change. The latest version will remain available on this page.

## Contact

For questions regarding Blood or this Privacy Policy, contact the developer through Blood's official Root community or support channel.
